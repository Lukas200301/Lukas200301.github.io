Motion test

Each lane moves at the same speed (pixels per second); the lower lanes only update every 2nd and 4th frame. Follow a UFO with your eyes: the sharper and smoother it stays, the better the panel's response time and refresh rate. Smearing behind it is ghosting, a bright halo is overshoot.
