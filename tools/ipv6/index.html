EUI-64 from a MAC

MAC address
/64 prefix for SLAAC

EUI-64 puts ff:fe in the middle of the MAC and flips the universal/local bit (7th bit of the first byte). Most systems now use random privacy addresses instead (RFC 4941 / 7217).

ULA prefix generator

A unique local /48 (fd00::/8) is your private IPv6 space, like 10.0.0.0/8. The 40-bit Global ID must be random so two networks that merge later do not collide. The RFC 4193 method hashes the current time and the EUI-64 of the MAC above with SHA-1.
