Protection tips

Ways to reduce tracking

Use a privacy-focused browser

Firefox with strict tracking protection, Brave, or Tor Browser for maximum anonymity.

Add privacy extensions

uBlock Origin, Privacy Badger or CanvasBlocker make fingerprinting harder.

Adjust browser settings

Block third-party cookies, disable JavaScript where you can, and use private windows.

Use a VPN

A VPN hides your IP address and location, one part of your digital fingerprint.
