Remove metadata

                                
Redraws the pixels on a canvas and saves a fresh file: EXIF, GPS, XMP, IPTC, comments and the embedded thumbnail are all dropped. The rotation from the EXIF orientation is baked into the pixels so the photo still shows the right way up.
