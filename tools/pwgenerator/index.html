What gets sent

  1. Hashed in your browserThe password becomes a SHA-1 hash.–
  2. Five characters sentOnly the start of the hash goes to the API.–
  3. Compared in your browserThe API returns every leaked hash that starts the same way, and your browser looks for a match.–

This is the k-anonymity range API, with padding switched on so even the size of the answer gives nothing away.
